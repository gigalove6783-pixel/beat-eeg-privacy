BEAT는 운동 상상 BCI가 기기 밖으로 보내는 특징 벡터나 왼쪽·오른쪽·정지 명령만 보고 도청자가 사용자를 얼마나 알아내는지 측정한다. 이 페이지의 수치에는 공격자가 언제 등록했는지가 함께 붙어 있다. 같은 날(within-day) 수치는 공격자가 같은 기록일 안에서 등록하고 시험한 값이고, 다른 날(cross-day) 수치는 보정일에 등록하고 배포일에 시험한 값이다.

      Does a brain–computer interface reveal who you are?

      A BCI turns imagined left- or right-hand movement into commands and often sends something off the device: a feature vector, a command, or both. We measured how well an eavesdropper can tell which of 9 people produced what was sent, and whether that can be reduced without hurting control. A sealed replication then re-tested the main claims on 54 people.

      Identity score = balanced accuracy of an attacker naming the right person. Chance is – for 9 users and – for 54; 1.00 means everyone is identified.

      Every result carries one of three evidence tiers (as in README.md): Confirmatory-sealed = decided by a rule committed before the held-out data were scored (seals 1, 3 and 4); Pre-registered estimate = fixed in a sealed plan and reported without a verdict (C's command values; L's and B's cross-day values; M's EQ/RR and utility values); Exploratory = not pre-registered, or computed on data that had already shaped the analysis (D, E, G, K, R). Every value also names its scenario, within-day or cross-day. Hover a number to see its source file.

      What this does not show

      • A 9-person closed set (BCI Competition IV 2a), not a population.
      • Arm control is a replay of recorded EEG, not closed-loop control with a person.
      • An attacker's failure is not anonymization.
      • A within-day number alone shows neither risk nor protection: cross-day values differ (README table).
      • Device compromise, identifying metadata and unobserved sensitive attributes are out of scope.
      • Replay processing time is not online latency.

      The study at a glance

      Eight experiments on one question. A–G use the 9 users of BCI Competition IV 2a; L repeats the key claims on 54 users; K reuses both datasets for command streams and two output defences. Within-day means session E runs 1–3 ↔ 4–6 on 2a and L's offline ↔ online phase of session 2; cross-day means 2a session T → E and L session 1 → 2. The attacker sees only what the device sends, trains its own classifiers and names the user; it never sees raw EEG or account metadata.

              Method and integrity

              • Pre-registered seal

                Plan, configs, code and all session-T choices were committed before session E was evaluated: –.

              • Independent attackers

                Logistic regression, RBF SVM and MLP, retrained on frozen outputs. Training-time adversary heads are reported, never used as evidence.

              • Failures reported

                H1 (1 calibration run: Riemannian – vs CSP –, p = –) and H2 were not supported and are reported as results.

              • Disclosed deviation

              • Replay checks

              Pre-registration timeline

              Each seal is a Git commit that fixed the plan, configuration, code and training-data-only choices before the corresponding held-out data were scored.
